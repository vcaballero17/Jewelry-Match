About

The idea

Most jewelry sites start with a product grid. Jewelry Match starts with the body. You say where a piece should live, then what shapes, materials and moods you are drawn to, and it returns four pieces from a small collection of twelve.

It is a guide and an archive, not a shop. There are no prices, no basket and no checkout.

How your edit is chosen

Each of the twelve pieces is tagged with one placement, one form, one material and one mood. When you answer the four questions, a piece earns points for every tag it shares with you. The four choices carry different weight, and together they add up to 100, so the total is already a percentage.

Points for each matching choice
ChoicePointsWhat it decides
Placement40Where the piece lives on the body. This is the core choice.
Form25The shape language: angular, liquid, delicate or organic.
Material / color20Silver, gold, emerald or yellow stone.
Mood15The atmosphere: quiet, sharp, romantic or after dark.

A worked example

The four highest scores are shown. If two pieces tie, the one that comes earlier in the collection is listed first, so the same answers always give the same order. If fewer than four pieces share your placement, the best remaining pieces fill the list and are marked as a weaker fit.

The explanation under each match only names choices that really matched. Nothing is guessed, and the matching never looks at your body, your looks or who you are. It only compares the four preferences you picked.

Your data

There are no accounts and no server. Your saved pieces and your latest match are stored in this browser only, so clearing your browser data removes them.

When you send your edit to a friend, the link carries your four answers after the #. Anyone who opens the link sees your four matches. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Images and AI

Every image on this site, including the Body Map photograph, was generated with OpenAI image generation in November 2026. The people shown are anonymous and the pieces are imaginary: they cannot be bought. The picture descriptions describe what each final image shows.

Jewelry Match was built as a university web design project, with AI assistance for the code and the images. The process, the prompts and the decisions are documented in the project files.

Accessibility

The site is built to work with a keyboard and a screen reader, to stay usable at 200% zoom and on a phone, and to respect a device's reduced-motion setting. The aim is WCAG 2.1 AA. If something does not work for you, it is a bug in the project.
