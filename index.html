How it works

  • The body comes first.

    You start with a place: ear, finger, hand, neckline or ankle. Everything else follows from there.

  • Four choices, weighted.

    Form, material and mood refine the match. Placement counts the most, because it decides what can be worn.

  • Every match explained.

    Each piece comes with a percentage and the exact choices it shares with you. See how the scoring works.
